What is GCSE?

The guided prompt structure used to build stronger Copilot prompts.

Goal

Define what you want Copilot to accomplish.

Context

Add patient, team, role, specialty, or workflow context to improve relevance.

Source

Specify what references, notes, chart details, or metrics Copilot should use.

Expectations

Describe structure, tone, length, and output quality requirements.

Microsoft terms disclaimer

Summary guidance for responsible use of generated prompts and responses.

Use and verification

AI-generated content may be inaccurate or incomplete. Review, validate, and edit outputs before relying on them or sharing them.

Data and privacy

Do not include confidential, regulated, or personal data unless your organization permits it and use complies with Microsoft Online Services Terms and privacy commitments.

Your responsibility

You are responsible for prompts, outputs, decisions, and compliance with applicable laws and organizational policy.

How to use this app

Follow the GCSE flow, fill placeholders, then copy and use your prompt in Copilot.

1. Work left to right

Select Goal, Goal Task, Context, Source, and Expectations.

2. Use role suggestions

At Context and Expectations steps, use nurse-type ideas to speed prompt building.

3. Fill placeholders

Type values for highlighted placeholders to personalize your prompt.

4. Review before use

Check for clinical accuracy and local policy alignment before operational use.
